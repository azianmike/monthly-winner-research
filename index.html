What passed. What remains uncertain.

An independent rebuild reproduces the supplied approximation. It does not validate the unobserved stocks or prove the result could have been achieved in a real account.

The numbers reconcile

All 346 purchases, observed rankings, and monthly portfolio values were recomputed from the frozen inputs. Totals match to within a cent: $8,204,466.91 and $2,258,566.73. All four supplied input checksums match. Fresh Yahoo checks also agree with the SPY, Apple, and Nvidia purchase prices; this is a cross-check against a shared upstream provider, not a second independent market feed.

The true monthly winner is unknown in 235 months

Only 111 rankings have complete price coverage within the supplied membership list. The $8.20M result belongs to the best-observed-stock approximation. A complete historical stock universe and corporate-action history are needed to test the original claim.

“No price” is broader than “went bankrupt”

The code values any purchased series ending before October 9, 2026 at zero. That includes acquisitions and inaccessible histories. It writes off 113 purchases in 72 stocks, originally costing $113,000. The historical line drops a holding only after its final quote.

Membership and adjustments need explicit assumptions

The model uses members at the previous month’s end. JCP was selected for December 2, 2013, its removal date in the supplied file. Four split corrections are documented heuristics; the archive lacks the daily corporate-action evidence needed to independently certify each one. Dividend and spin-off returns follow the provider’s adjustments.

The annual percentages are not a promise of yearly gains

17.84% and 11.11% are money-weighted annual returns, accounting for the dates money went in. They use a 365.25-day year. Standard Excel XIRR uses 365 days and gives 17.83% and 11.11%. The workbook’s return cells are fixed numeric formulas, so they will not recalculate if the underlying results change.

How the experiment works

Rank the preceding calendar month’s total return among members at signal month-end. Buy the observed leader at the next month’s first trading-day open; ties use ascending ticker order. Repeat winners receive another $1,000. Hold purchases even after they leave the S&P 500. The comparison buys SPY, an exchange-traded fund tracking the S&P 500, at those same opens.

Both sides use fractional shares and reinvested distributions, with no taxes, commissions, spread, or slippage. SPY fund costs are embedded. The adjusted open is open × adjusted close ÷ close; adjusted units are bookkeeping units, not literal shares. No selection signal comes after its buy date. That chronological check does not prove the reconstructed data were available in this exact form at the time.
